FAQ

Your Questions, Answered

Everything you need to know before embarking on your adventure.

What's included in a sharing tour package?

Sharing tour packages include transportation (AC vehicle), accommodation, meals as specified in the itinerary, guide services, and all entry fees to attractions. You'll be traveling with a small group of fellow adventurers!

How many people are in a sharing group?

Our sharing groups typically have 8–15 travelers. This keeps the experience intimate and fun, while making it cost-effective for everyone. Small enough to feel personal, big enough to be exciting!

Can I customize a private tour completely?

Absolutely! Private tours are 100% customizable. You choose the dates, destinations, duration, accommodation type, and activities. Our team will design a bespoke itinerary just for you. Contact us to start planning!

What is the best season to visit?

October to May is ideal for all three destinations. October–November offers crystal-clear skies and mountain views. March–May brings blooming rhododendrons. Monsoon (June–September) makes Yelbong lush and dramatic but can affect road conditions.

Is prior trekking experience needed for Yelbong?

No! Our Yelbong treks are designed for all fitness levels. We offer beginner-friendly trails as well as more challenging routes for experienced trekkers. Our guides ensure everyone stays safe and has an amazing time.

How do I book a tour?

Simply fill out our enquiry form below or reach out via WhatsApp/phone. Our team will get back to you within 24 hours with package details, availability, and pricing. A 30% advance is required to confirm your booking.

What is the cancellation policy?

Cancellations made 15+ days before departure receive a full refund. 7–14 days: 50% refund. Less than 7 days: no refund. We strongly recommend travel insurance. Special considerations are made for emergency situations.

Do you arrange permits for restricted areas?

Yes! Certain areas in Sikkim require special permits (ILP). We handle all permit formalities on your behalf — you just need to provide the required documents (ID/passport copies) when booking. Stress-free travel guaranteed!
